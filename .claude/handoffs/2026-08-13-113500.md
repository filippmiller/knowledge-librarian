```
question + audience
  → classifyScenario (expandAbbreviations first: КЗАГС → «…ЗАГС Санкт-Петербурга»)
  → hybrid search: scenarioKey IS NULL OR IN ancestors   ← leak surface
  → filterCrossInstitutionEvidence(question, scenarioKey, …)
```
